04Compliance and Safety Rails

Built to keep Summit Air on the right side of the rules on every call and text.

⏰ Quiet hours on outbound

The AI answers inbound calls any hour, but every outbound follow-up text and call is gated to daytime windows. A homeowner never gets a sales text at midnight.

🛑 Honors opt-out instantly

Any "stop", "take me off your list", or "leave me alone" in any wording ends contact at once across calls and texts. Carrier-level STOP is respected too.

🔁 Hard caps on follow-up

Follow-up stops after a set number of touches. There is no path to endless messaging, and a booked job stops all nurture immediately.

🧾 Full audit trail

Every call recorded and transcribed, every text logged in the CRM, every booking stamped on the calendar. Nothing happens off the record.
