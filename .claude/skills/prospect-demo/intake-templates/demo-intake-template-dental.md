# Demo Intake Template — Dental Practices

Reusable email template for general dentistry, cosmetic, pediatric, ortho, and specialty dental prospects. Tuned for the reality of dental front desk calls: insurance verification, dental anxiety, emergency triage, and the recall game.

---

**Subject:** Quick info to build [Practice]'s voice agent demo

Hey [Name],

Great talking. To build a demo that sounds like the front desk at [Practice Name] — handling new patient calls, insurance questions, dental emergencies — I need a few things. The more detail here, the sharper the demo.

**What I need:**

**1. The basics**
- Practice name + locations
- Type: general / cosmetic / pediatric / orthodontic / endodontic / periodontic / oral surgery / multi-specialty
- Providers (Dr. names + specialties)
- Website

**2. What should the agent handle?** (Pick all that apply)
- New patient inquiries
- Existing patient appointments (cleaning, exam, follow-up)
- Dental emergencies (severe pain, broken tooth, trauma)
- Insurance verification questions
- Treatment plan questions
- Billing / payment questions
- Recall / hygiene rebook
- Transfer to a human

**3. EMERGENCY TRIAGE** ⚠️
"I broke a tooth," "I'm in severe pain," "My kid got hit in the mouth at soccer" — agent should:
- (A) Book the next emergency slot today (criteria for "today")
- (B) Take info, dentist or coordinator calls back within X minutes
- (C) Triage by severity — broken vs. severe pain vs. swelling vs. trauma have different paths

What's an after-hours dental emergency policy? (ER referral, on-call dentist, callback first thing?)

**4. Insurance — the conversation that eats your front desk** 💰
- In-network with which insurers? (Delta Dental, MetLife, Cigna, Aetna, BCBS, etc.)
- Out-of-network policy: file claims for patient, accept assignment, patient pays upfront and gets reimbursed?
- Cash-pay rates?
- In-house membership plan? (price + benefits)
- How should the agent handle "Do you take my insurance?":
  - (A) Verify on the call (slow but accurate)
  - (B) Take insurance info, verify after, call back
  - (C) General script ("we accept most major PPOs, we'll verify your benefits before your visit")

**5. Booking system**
- Dentrix / Eaglesoft / Open Dental / Curve / Practice-Web / NexHealth / Weave / phone-only
- Provider-specific scheduling rules
- New patient first-visit flow — exam + X-rays + cleaning together, or split?
- Appointment types: hygiene (60 min), exam (30 min), emergency (45 min), etc.

**6. Top 10 FAQ**
- "Do you take my insurance?" → ...
- "How much is a cleaning without insurance?" → ...
- "Are you accepting new patients?" → ...
- "How long is the first visit?" → ...
- "Do you do payment plans / CareCredit?" → ...
- "Do you see kids?" / "What's the youngest you see?"
- "Do you do [specific procedure — Invisalign, implants, veneers]?"
- "Do you offer sedation?"

**7. Anxious patients**
A real chunk of dental callers are nervous. How should the agent reassure them?
- Mention sedation options? (IV / oral / nitrous)
- Specific provider known for anxious patients?
- Just be calm and warm, no scripted reassurance?
- Specific language to avoid (the word "drill" sets people off)

**8. When to transfer to a human?**
(complex insurance verification, treatment plan negotiation, missed appointment fee disputes, complaints, refunds)

**9. Recall / hygiene rebook**
Should the agent proactively help patients rebook if they're due for a 6-month cleaning? Or strictly inbound only?

**10. Personality**
- Name
- Tone: calm + warm + professional (anxiety is the #1 thing to manage)
- Voice: female tends to test better in dental, but no rule
- Accent

**11. Languages**
Spanish-speaking patients are a huge slice. Bilingual agent or transfer?

**12. Existing front desk call recordings** ⭐
3–5 real calls — new patient, insurance question, emergency, recall — will make the demo sound like your team.

**13. Send if available**
- New patient packet / intake forms
- List of insurances accepted (in-network)
- Cash fee schedule
- Membership plan one-pager (if you have one)
