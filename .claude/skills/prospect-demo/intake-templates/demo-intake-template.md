# Demo Intake Template

Reusable email template for gathering info from prospects before building a voice agent demo. Swap in the prospect's name and send as-is.

---

**Subject:** Quick info needed to build your voice agent demo

Hey [Name],

Great connecting today. To build a demo that actually sounds like it knows your business from the first call, I need a few things from you. The more you give me here, the sharper the demo will be.

**What I need:**

**1. Who is the voice agent for?**
- (A) Your company — answering inbound calls from prospects, qualifying them, booking demos/consults
- (B) Your customers — a white-label agent your customers would use to answer their own calls
- (C) Both

**2. What should the agent do on a call?** (Pick all that apply)
- Answer FAQs about your services/pricing
- Qualify the caller (budget, use case, timeline, fit)
- Book appointments on your calendar
- Transfer to a team member when needed
- Collect contact info / create a lead record
- Something else?

**3. Top 5–10 questions your callers actually ask — with the answers you'd want the agent to give.**
Example format:
- "What does it cost?" → "Plans start at $X/mo with no contracts..."
- "How long does setup take?" → "Usually 2–3 weeks depending on..."
- "Do you integrate with [tool]?" → "Yes, we connect with..."

Don't overthink the wording — rough answers are fine, I'll polish them.

**4. When should the agent transfer to a human?**
(e.g., pricing negotiation, technical deep-dives, enterprise deals, anything outside its lane)

**5. Agent personality**
- Name
- Tone: professional / friendly / casual / high-energy
- Male or female voice
- Any accent preference

**6. Existing sales scripts, objection handling, or call frameworks**
Even rough notes from your team help. If you have a "how we handle pricing pushback" doc or similar, send it.

**7. Call recordings or transcripts from past sales/support calls** ⭐
This is the biggest unlock. If you can share 2–5 real call recordings or transcripts, the agent will sound 10x more like your team from day one. Anonymize anything sensitive — I just need the conversation flow.

**8. Business basics** (in case we didn't fully cover it)
- One-sentence pitch for what you do
- Who your ideal customer is
- Your pricing tiers (even approximate)
- Website URL

---

Just reply to this email or drop it in a Google Doc and share it — whatever's easier.

Talk soon,
[Your name]
