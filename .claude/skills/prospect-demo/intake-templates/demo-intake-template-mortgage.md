# Demo Intake Template — Mortgage & Lending

Reusable email template for mortgage brokers, retail lenders, and bankers. Tuned for the reality of mortgage calls: TILA/RESPA compliance guardrails, qualifying questions, and the fact that loan officers are never at their desk when leads call.

---

**Subject:** Quick info to build [Company]'s voice agent demo

Hey [Name],

Great talking. To build a demo that sounds like your team's inside loan officer assistant — capturing new purchase leads, fielding refi inquiries, qualifying borrowers without crossing compliance lines — I need a few things.

Heads up: mortgage voice agents are different from other industries. The agent will NEVER quote rates, promise approval, or talk specific loan terms without the right disclosures. It captures, qualifies, and routes. Everything below is built around that.

**What I need:**

**1. The basics**
- Company name + branches
- Type: broker / banker / retail lender / correspondent / credit union
- States licensed
- Loan products: conventional / FHA / VA / USDA / jumbo / non-QM / HELOC / refi / reverse / DSCR / other
- Company NMLS#
- Website

**2. What should the agent handle?** (Pick all that apply)
- New purchase prequalification inquiries
- Refinance inquiries
- Existing application status (route to LOA / processor)
- Realtor partner referrals
- Rate / program inquiries
- Transfer to a loan officer
- Take a message

**3. Compliance guardrails** ⚠️
The agent must NEVER:
- Quote specific interest rates (rates change daily, TILA/TRID disclosures required)
- Promise approval or pre-approval
- Discuss specific loan terms without proper disclosures
- Imply a loan estimate is being given

How should it handle "What's the rate today?":
- (A) Generic ranges only, with disclaimer ("rates change daily, an LO can give you a quote with current pricing")
- (B) Soft transfer to LO — "let me grab a loan officer"
- (C) Take info, LO calls back with a custom rate quote

**4. Qualifying questions for new leads**
- Purchase or refi?
- Property type: primary, second home, investment
- Estimated price / loan amount
- Estimated credit (excellent 740+, good 680–739, fair 620–679, building <620)
- Down payment available
- W2 / self-employed
- Timeline (under contract? actively shopping? "just curious"?)
- VA eligible? First-time buyer? Other specialty programs?

What's the threshold for a live transfer to an LO vs. capture-and-callback?

**5. Loan officer routing**
- Solo LO or team?
- Routing by state, loan type, language, lead source?
- LOS: Encompass / LendingPad / Calyx / Floify / Arive / Loan Sifter / other
- POS for application: Floify / SimpleNexus / Maxwell / Blend / direct LOS

**6. Top FAQs**
- "Are you taking new applications?"
- "What programs do you offer?"
- "Do you do FHA / VA / USDA / jumbo / non-QM?"
- "What's your minimum credit score?"
- "How long does the process take?"
- "Do you have closing cost assistance?"
- "Do you do down payment assistance?"
- "How fast can you close?"

**7. Realtor partner referrals**
A big chunk of mortgage volume is realtor-referred. Should the agent:
- Capture which realtor / source sent the lead
- Priority-route realtor referrals to the LO who has the relationship
- Send a courtesy notification to the referring agent

**8. When to transfer to a human?**
(Qualified borrower ready to apply, realtor referral, current client status questions, complaints, complex scenarios)

**9. After-hours behavior**
Most refi calls come evenings; purchase calls come weekends when buyers are house-hunting. Should the agent:
- Capture, LO calls back next business day
- Page on-call LO for hot leads (criteria?)

**10. Personality**
- Name
- Tone: trustworthy + professional + patient (these are big decisions; callers want hand-holding, not pressure)
- Male or female
- Accent

**11. Languages**
Spanish is a huge slice of mortgage volume. Bilingual agent or transfer to a Spanish-speaking LO?

**12. Existing intake call recordings** ⭐
3–5 real calls — purchase inquiry, refi, realtor referral, status check — will make the demo sound like your team.

**13. Send if available**
- Loan program one-pagers
- Intake / application form
- Compliance scripts your firm uses for "what's the rate" calls
- Preferred realtor partner list (for routing)
