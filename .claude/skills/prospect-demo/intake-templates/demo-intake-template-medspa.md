# Demo Intake Template — Med Spa & Aesthetics

Reusable email template for med spa, aesthetic clinic, cosmetic derm, and plastic surgery prospects. Tuned for the reality of aesthetic calls: consults, treatment bookings, pricing questions, and the medical guardrails that keep the agent safe.

---

**Subject:** Quick info to build [Practice]'s voice agent demo

Hey [Name],

Great talking today. To build a demo that sounds like the front desk at [Practice Name] on a good day — booking consults, answering treatment questions, handling pricing — I need a few things. The more detail here, the sharper the demo.

**What I need:**

**1. The basics**
- Practice name + locations
- Type: med spa / aesthetic clinic / cosmetic dermatology / plastic surgery / wellness + aesthetics
- Provider mix: MD-led / NP-led / RN injectors / esthetician services
- Website + Instagram (Instagram matters for vibe)

**2. What should the agent handle?** (Pick all that apply)
- Book consults
- Book treatment appointments (returning clients)
- Answer treatment FAQs (Botox, filler, laser, body contouring, microneedling, etc.)
- Quote pricing
- Financing questions (Cherry, CareCredit, Affirm)
- Pre/post-treatment care questions
- Reschedule / cancel
- Transfer to a human
- Take a message

**3. Booking system**
- System: Aesthetic Record / Boulevard / Vagaro / Mindbody / Symplast / Nextech / phone-only / other
- Which providers can the agent book directly vs. require coordinator confirmation?
- Consult vs treatment durations
- Deposit / cancellation / no-show policy the agent should mention

**4. Top treatments — with the prices and answers callers ask**
Example format:
- Botox: "$13/unit, most clients use 30–50 units, results show in 7–14 days, lasts 3–4 months"
- Lip filler: "Starts at $750 for half a syringe, $1,200 for a full syringe"
- Laser hair removal: "Package of 6 sessions, $1,200 full legs, $400 underarms"
- Morpheus8: "$1,500 per session, package of 3 recommended"

Don't overthink the wording — rough numbers are fine.

**5. Pricing scripting — where's the line?**
- (A) Give specific prices over the phone
- (B) Give starting prices, full pricing at consult
- (C) "We don't quote pricing on the phone, but I can book you a free consult"

**6. Provider preferences**
Some patients have strong preferences. How should the agent handle:
- "Can I book with [Dr./NP name]?"
- "I want a female injector"
- "I want whoever has the most experience with [treatment]"
- New patient with no preference — does it pick or ask?

**7. Medical safety guardrails** ⚠️
The agent must NEVER:
- Promise specific outcomes
- Diagnose anything
- Give medical advice
- Confirm someone is a candidate for a treatment

How should it handle questions like:
- "Will Botox work for my forehead lines?"
- "Can I get filler if I'm pregnant or breastfeeding?"
- "I have [condition] — can I do this treatment?"

Default is soft escalate to consult — let me know if you want different.

**8. When to transfer to a human?**
(complications/side effects, billing disputes, anything medical, complaints, refunds)

**9. Existing client vs. new client**
Should the agent recognize existing clients (by phone match) and route differently?
(e.g., maintenance Botox rebook is a 2-min call; new consult is 10 min)

**10. Personality**
- Name (warm-sounding names work — Sophia, Ava, Olivia, Sienna)
- Tone: warm + professional / luxurious + composed / friendly + knowledgeable
- Voice: female is the norm in this space, but no rule
- Accent

**11. Languages**
Should the agent handle Spanish callers? Spanish-fluent injectors increasingly important.

**12. Existing front desk call recordings** ⭐
3–5 real recordings — booking calls, pricing questions, FAQ calls — will make the demo sound 10x more like your team. Anonymize anything sensitive.

**13. Anything else**
House-favorite treatments, signature packages, current promos, anything that makes your spa yours.

---

Just reply or drop it in a Google Doc. If you can attach your treatment menu PDF, current promo flyers, and a screenshot of your booking system, even better.

Talk soon,
[Your name]
