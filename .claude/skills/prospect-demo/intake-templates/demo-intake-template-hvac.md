# Demo Intake Template — HVAC & Home Services

Reusable email template for HVAC, plumbing, electrical, and broader home-service prospects. Tuned for the reality of dispatch calls: emergency triage, service area validation, and the difference between "AC won't cool" and "no AC and it's 95 degrees."

---

**Subject:** Quick info to build [Company]'s voice agent demo

Hey [Name],

Great talking. To build a demo that sounds like dispatch at [Company Name] handling a 95-degree afternoon — emergencies, maintenance bookings, quote requests — I need a few things. The more detail here, the sharper the demo.

**What I need:**

**1. The basics**
- Company name + service area (zip codes, counties, or radius from a city)
- Services: AC / heating / both / + plumbing / + electrical / + other
- Residential / commercial / both
- Hours including after-hours / weekend / holiday coverage
- Website

**2. What should the agent handle?** (Pick all that apply)
- Emergency service requests (no AC, no heat, leak, no hot water)
- Schedule routine maintenance / tune-ups
- Quote requests for new install / replacement
- Existing job status (when's my tech arriving?)
- Membership / service plan inquiries
- Take a message after hours
- Transfer to a human (dispatcher, sales, manager)

**3. EMERGENCY TRIAGE — the most important question** ⚠️
When someone calls and says "my AC is broken and it's 95 degrees" — what should the agent do?
- (A) Book the next available emergency slot today
- (B) Page on-call tech immediately
- (C) Take info and have dispatcher call back within X minutes
- Different rules for active leak / no heat in winter / no hot water?

**4. Service area**
- Zip codes / counties / radius you cover
- What to say to out-of-area callers — decline politely, refer to a partner, take their info anyway?

**5. Dispatch + scheduling system**
- ServiceTitan / Housecall Pro / Jobber / FieldEdge / Sera / phone + paper / other
- Can the agent book directly into the calendar, or route to a dispatcher?
- Service window length: 2-hour, 4-hour, AM/PM, all-day?

**6. Pricing scripting**
- Service call / diagnostic fee — disclose on call or "we'll discuss when tech arrives"?
- Membership / maintenance plan price + benefits
- "How much to replace my AC?" — give a range, soft transfer to sales, or always require an in-home estimate?
- Financing offered? (GoodLeap, Synchrony, etc.)

**7. After-hours behavior**
Nights, weekends, holidays — should the agent:
- Take messages only (call back next business day)
- Dispatch on-call tech for true emergencies (criteria?)
- Book into next morning's emergency slot
- Different by service type (no heat in January = page; tune-up = next day)

**8. Top 10 FAQ**
- "Do you service my area?" → ...
- "How fast can you get here?" → ...
- "Do you charge for estimates?" → ...
- "Are your techs licensed and insured?" → ...
- "Do you offer financing?" → ...
- "Do you do free second opinions?" → ...
- "What brands do you install / service?" → ...

**9. When to transfer to a human?**
(commercial bids, warranty disputes, complaints, large replacement quotes)

**10. Personality**
- Name
- Tone: trustworthy + reassuring (callers are stressed, often hot or cold or wet)
- Male or female
- Accent

**11. Languages**
Spanish callers are a huge slice of home-service volume. Bilingual agent or transfer to a Spanish-speaking dispatcher?

**12. Existing call recordings from dispatch** ⭐
3–5 real calls — emergency, scheduling, FAQ — will make the demo sound like your office. Even if recorded on a phone speaker, send them.

**13. Send if available**
- Service area map
- Pricing sheet / diagnostic fee
- Membership plan one-pager
- After-hours protocol if you have one written down

Talk soon,
[Your name]
