# Demo Intake Template — Roofing & Restoration

Reusable email template for roofing contractors, exterior contractors, and storm restoration prospects. Tuned for the reality of roofing calls: storm-driven volume spikes, insurance claim navigation, active leak emergencies, and "free inspection" lead capture.

---

**Subject:** Quick info to build [Company]'s voice agent demo

Hey [Name],

Great talking. To build a demo that sounds like dispatch at [Company Name] — handling storm damage calls, free inspection requests, insurance claim help — I need a few things. The more detail here, the sharper the demo.

**What I need:**

**1. The basics**
- Company name + service area (zip codes, counties, or radius)
- Services: roofing only / + siding / + gutters / + windows / + restoration (water/fire/mold)
- Residential / commercial / both
- Insurance work, retail, or both (and what split)
- Website

**2. What should the agent handle?** (Pick all that apply)
- Storm damage / active leak emergencies
- Free inspection requests
- Insurance claim help / supplements
- Existing job status (when's the crew arriving?)
- New construction / re-roof quote requests
- Repair estimates
- Take a message after hours
- Transfer to a human (sales rep, project manager, owner)

**3. EMERGENCY TRIAGE** ⚠️
"My roof is leaking right now" or "Tree fell on my house" — agent should:
- (A) Dispatch tarp / emergency crew immediately
- (B) Book emergency tarp service for today
- (C) Take info, sales rep calls back within X minutes
- Different rules for active leak vs. tree damage vs. wind damage with no immediate water?

**4. Insurance work — the big differentiator** 💰
Half of roofing demand is insurance-driven. The agent should know how to talk about it.
- Does the firm work with insurance claims, retail-only, or both?
- For insurance leads, what should the agent ask?
  - Have you filed a claim yet?
  - Has an adjuster been out?
  - What's your deductible?
  - When did the storm happen?
- How should it phrase "Will my insurance cover this?" — never promises, but how do you want it framed?

**5. Service area**
- Zip codes / counties / radius
- Out-of-area policy: decline politely, refer to a partner, or take their info anyway for future expansion?

**6. Booking + dispatch system**
- AccuLynx / JobNimbus / Roofr / CompanyCam / Leap / phone + paper / other
- Can the agent book inspections directly, or always route to a sales rep?
- Inspection appointment length / window

**7. Top 10 FAQ**
- "Do you work with insurance?" → ...
- "Is the inspection free?" → ...
- "How long does a roof replacement take?" → ...
- "Do you offer financing?" → ...
- "Are you licensed and insured?" → ...
- "What's your warranty?" → ...
- "What manufacturers / shingles do you carry?" → ...
- "Can you match my insurance estimate?" → ...
- "Do you do small repairs?" → ...

**8. Storm response mode**
After a hailstorm, call volume can 10x for two weeks. Should the agent:
- Capture aggressively, book everyone for free inspection (default in storm season)
- Pre-screen by storm date / damage age / address in impact zone
- Different script during active storm response vs. quiet months

**9. Pricing scripting**
- Free inspection — give the script
- "How much for a new roof?" — give a range, soft transfer to sales, or always require an in-home inspection?
- Repairs vs. replacement — does the agent quote ranges?
- Financing partners (GreenSky, Hearth, Service Finance, etc.)

**10. When to transfer to a human?**
(commercial bids, complaints, warranty disputes, supplement / insurance disputes, active customer mid-project, anything outside its lane)

**11. Personality**
- Name
- Tone: confident + no-BS + reassuring (callers are often stressed, dealing with damage)
- Male or female (male tends to test better in roofing/exterior, but no rule)
- Accent

**12. Languages**
Spanish callers are a huge slice of exterior contractor demand — both homeowners and crews. Bilingual agent or transfer?

**13. Existing call recordings from your office** ⭐
3–5 real calls — storm leak, insurance claim question, FAQ, scheduling — gold for tuning the demo.

**14. Send if available**
- Service area map
- Inspection process overview
- Insurance work brochure / supplement workflow
- Manufacturer / financing partner one-pagers
- Any active storm campaign you're running

[Your name]
