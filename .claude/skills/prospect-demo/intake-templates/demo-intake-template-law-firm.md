# Demo Intake Template — Law Firms (Personal Injury Focus)

Reusable email template for personal injury, mass tort, and adjacent contingency-fee firms. Tuned for the reality of legal intake: high-stakes screening, statute-of-limitations checks, and the firm guardrails that keep the agent from giving legal advice.

If the firm is transactional (estate, business, family) the shape changes — ask me and I'll tweak.

---

**Subject:** Quick info to build [Firm]'s voice agent demo

Hey [Name],

Great talking. To build a demo that sounds like intake at [Firm Name] — handling new accident calls, screening case viability, getting the right people to the right attorney — I need a few things.

Heads up: voice agents in legal play different from other industries. The agent will NEVER give legal advice or promise case viability. It collects, screens, and routes. Everything below is built around that.

**What I need:**

**1. The basics**
- Firm name + offices
- Practice areas: PI only / + workers comp / + mass tort / + criminal / + other
- States licensed in (and which counties matter most)
- Website

**2. What should the agent handle?** (Pick all that apply)
- New PI intake (auto, slip-and-fall, dog bite, premises, medical malpractice, etc.)
- Existing client status updates → route to case manager
- Press / media inquiries → transfer or message
- Vendor / opposing counsel → transfer or take message
- Spam / cold sales → screen out politely
- Referrals from other attorneys → priority routing

**3. NEW INTAKE — the core flow** 📋
What should the agent collect on a new accident call?
- Type of accident (auto, slip-and-fall, etc.)
- When it happened (statute of limitations gate)
- Where (state/county for jurisdiction)
- Any injuries — and were they treated by a doctor?
- Police report filed?
- Insurance involved (theirs, other party's)?
- At fault for the accident?
- Already represented by another attorney?

How should the agent handle the call?
- (A) Collect everything, route to intake specialist for callback
- (B) Live transfer to intake specialist immediately if available
- (C) Hybrid — live transfer during business hours, full intake + callback after hours

**4. Case-screening boundaries** ⚠️
The agent must NEVER:
- Tell a caller they "have a case"
- Estimate case value or settlement
- Give legal advice
- Promise outcomes

How should it handle "Do I have a case?" — soft handoff to intake specialist? Specific phrasing you want?

**5. Disqualifiers — auto-decline or always escalate?**
Some firms decline:
- Cases past statute of limitations or close to it
- Unrepresented but at-fault (no recoverable insurance)
- No medical treatment received
- Soft tissue only
- Claims under a $X threshold

What's your firm's policy? Should the agent decline politely and refer out, or always escalate to a human regardless?

**6. After-hours**
Most PI calls come at the worst time — night, weekend, right after the accident. Agent should:
- (A) Full intake, attorney calls back first thing in the morning
- (B) Page on-call attorney for serious cases (criteria for "serious"?)
- (C) Hybrid

**7. When to transfer immediately?**
(Caller still at the scene, in the hospital, in custody, active emergency, prior client of the firm calling about a critical issue)

**8. Languages**
- English only?
- Spanish-speaking caller — bilingual agent, or hold + transfer to bilingual intake specialist?

**9. Existing client routing**
- Case management software: Litify / MyCase / Filevine / SmartAdvocate / Clio Grow / other
- Should the agent verify caller identity (DOB, case number) before discussing case info?
- Status update calls — agent answers basic ("hearing date," "settlement check status") or always routes to case manager?

**10. Personality**
- Name
- Tone: empathetic + professional + calm — never sales-y, never aggressive. Callers are often hurting, scared, or angry.
- Male or female
- Accent

**11. Existing intake call recordings** ⭐
3–5 real intake calls — auto accident, slip-and-fall, follow-up — will make the demo sound like your intake team. Anonymize anything sensitive (caller name, case details).

**12. Send if available**
- Intake form / case acceptance criteria
- Disqualifier checklist (if your firm has one)
- Fee agreement summary (one-liner the agent can paraphrase)
- Referral partners for cases you decline
