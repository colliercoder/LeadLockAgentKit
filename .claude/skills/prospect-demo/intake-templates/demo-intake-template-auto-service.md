# Demo Intake Template — Auto Service & Repair

Reusable email template for auto repair shops, dealer service departments, tire shops, and specialty mechanics. Tuned for the reality of service calls: status updates eat the front desk, breakdown triage matters, and trust is the whole game.

---

**Subject:** Quick info to build [Shop]'s voice agent demo

Hey [Name],

Great talking. To build a demo that sounds like your service writer on a Monday morning — booking oil changes, handling status calls, triaging "my car won't start" — I need a few things.

**What I need:**

**1. The basics**
- Shop name + locations
- Type: independent repair / dealer service department / tire shop / specialty (transmission, body, performance, EV) / mixed
- Brands serviced: foreign / domestic / all / specific (BMW only, Honda/Toyota, etc.)
- Hours, weekend coverage
- Website

**2. What should the agent handle?** (Pick all that apply)
- Appointment booking (oil change, brakes, diagnostic, tires, alignment)
- Status updates ("is my car ready?")
- Estimate requests
- Towing / breakdown / "my car won't start"
- Recall service inquiries
- Tire quotes (size lookup)
- Transfer to a service advisor
- Take a message

**3. BREAKDOWN TRIAGE** 🚗
"I'm broken down on the side of the road" or "Car won't start in my driveway" — agent should:
- (A) Recommend they call AAA / their tow service, drop the car at your shop
- (B) Connect with a preferred tow partner you have a relationship with
- (C) Take info, advisor calls them within X minutes
- "Check engine light just came on" — same-day diagnostic vs. next available?
- "Brakes are grinding" — urgent vs. next available?

**4. STATUS UPDATE CALLS — the volume eater**
"Is my car ready?" calls are 30%+ of front desk volume in some shops. Should the agent:
- (A) Look up status in the SMS / DMS and answer directly ("Yes, ready for pickup" or "Still waiting on parts")
- (B) Take info, advisor calls back
- (C) Always transfer to advisor

If (A), what system? (Tekmetric / Mitchell1 / Shopmonkey / AutoLeap / Shop-Ware / dealer DMS)

**5. Service menu pricing**
- Oil change tiers (conventional / synthetic blend / full synthetic / diesel)
- Brake job ranges (front pads, full brake job, rotors)
- Diagnostic fee
- Alignment
- Tire rotation
- "It depends on your vehicle" defaults — does the agent quote ranges or push to drop-off?

**6. Booking system**
- Tekmetric / Mitchell1 SE / Shopmonkey / AutoLeap / Shop-Ware / Protractor / dealer DMS / phone-only
- Service advisor schedule (book by advisor or any-available)
- Drop-off / wait / appointment-only?
- Loaner / shuttle / Uber credit policy

**7. Top FAQs**
- "How much for an oil change?"
- "Do you work on [brand]?"
- "Do you take walk-ins?"
- "Do you offer financing for major repairs?" (Snap / Sunbit / Affirm / Synchrony Car Care)
- "Can I bring my own parts?"
- "Do you do alignments?"
- "Do you have loaners?"
- "How long will the repair take?"
- "Do you honor [warranty company]?"

**8. When to transfer to an advisor?**
(Estimate over $X threshold, complaint, warranty work, fleet customer, hybrid/EV specifics, performance / mod work)

**9. Trust posture**
Auto repair has a trust problem industry-wide. How does your shop differentiate?
- Free inspections / digital DVI?
- Warranty on all work?
- "We'll show you the old part"?
- Specific certifications (ASE, AAA-approved, OEM-certified)?

The agent should weave these naturally into FAQ answers.

**10. Personality**
- Name
- Tone: friendly + honest + no-pressure (lean hard into trust — auto repair has a sales-y reputation)
- Male or female
- Accent

**11. Languages**
Spanish is huge in auto service. Bilingual agent or transfer?

**12. Existing call recordings from the service writer's desk** ⭐
3–5 real calls — booking, status check, estimate, breakdown — will make the demo sound like your shop.

**13. Send if available**
- Service menu / pricing sheet
- Brands / specialties served
- Financing partner one-pagers
- Loaner / shuttle policy

Talk soon,
[Your name]
