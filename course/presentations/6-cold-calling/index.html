Book it

Two times. Pick one.

Offer
‘2:00 or 4:30, which one?’
Confirm
‘You're in Dallas, so Central?’
Invite
Name, email, best cell
Close
‘I own the agency. It's me calling back.’
Never ask 'when works for you?'. Give two times. Get the time zone from their listing before you call. Confirm once, then get off the phone.
Objections

You'll hear these daily.

‘Not interested.’

‘We have a service.’

‘People want a human.’

‘Tried one. It sucked.’

‘We don't miss calls.’

‘Just email me.’

Every answer ends the same: just hear it.
Leadlock.ai
Don't argue. Ask one question, then go back to the demo. Service: 'I'm not asking you to cancel. Does it book the job?' Email: 'It's a voice thing. Two minutes and you'll know.'
Two lines that flip calls

Ask. Then reframe.

“Is it the AI part, or just not a right-now thing?”
“It's not AI or you. It's AI or voicemail.”
Line one for 'not interested'. Their answer tells you what to say next. Line two for 'people want a human'. It only picks up when they can't. Most owners still answer their own phone. Good. Keep it that way.
