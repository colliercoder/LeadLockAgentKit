The owner's email

TREG: one key, many email finders.

treg

What it does

Give it a business website or name. It finds the owner's name, then their work email.

Published price, per hit

$0.00049 to $0.15

Depends on which provider answers. New accounts get $1.00 free, once.

treg.to/search · find work email
treg.to catalog, 2026-10-20: treg.people.email.find $0.000495 to $0.15 per hit; treg.people.search $0 to $0.30 per hit; $1.00 free once for new verified accounts.
TREG routes one request to dozens of email-finder providers. You don't sign up for each provider. One token, pay per lookup. Prices are per hit, fractions of a cent on the cheap providers.

Leadlock Lead Finder: the whole run, start to finish.

    1. Facebook Ad Library  -> local businesses running ads right now
    2. Google Maps          -> every business of that type in the city
    3. Match + dedupe       -> one row per business (by website, then phone, then name)
    4. Owner + email        -> owner name (Treg), owner email (patterns + Reoon, then Treg)
    5. CSV                  -> output/<niche>-<city>-<state>-<time>-ready.csv

Nothing is ever emailed, messaged or uploaded anywhere. The CSV is the end.
