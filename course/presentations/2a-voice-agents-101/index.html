Pick the industry

The 3-question test.

Does winning the phone actually win the job?

Lots of inbound calls

Dozens a day, not a handful.

The owner's own phone rings

Not a national call center that answers for them.

The customer picks who to call

Not an insurer or broker assigning the work.

Three yeses: sell there. One no: move on.
