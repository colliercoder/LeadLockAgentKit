Each folder in .claude/skills is a skill. Say 'help' to see them. The big ones: build-agent, prospect-demo, prompt-tuner, call-audit, knowledge-base-builder, talking-website. learnings is the kit's memory. Claude reads it before work; add-to-learnings saves what worked and what broke. git pull for updates. Your keys and add-ons are never touched.
