What I call it

Shadow boxing.

Fight the round in your head before you step in the ring.
Maltz, Psycho-Cybernetics

“Your nervous system cannot tell the difference between an imagined experience and a ‘real’ experience.”

Maxwell Maltz, Psycho-Cybernetics, chapter 3 (Imagination). 'Shadow boxing' is Dan's name for the practice.
Boxers shadow box before the fight. Same idea for a sales call. Maltz's point: vivid rehearsal trains you almost like the real thing. By the time you dial, it's the second time you've had the call.
Shadow boxing a sales call

Rehearse it. Then dial.

02

03

Maltz's daily version: the Theater of Your Mind. Picture it on a screen, in detail.
Maxwell Maltz, Psycho-Cybernetics, chapter 3: the daily 'Theater of Your Mind' imagination practice.
Leadlock.ai
Two minutes before every call or demo. Eyes closed is fine. Spend most of it on the objections, not the easy parts. Maltz suggests a daily session. Start with five minutes, then dial.
A Happy Pocket Full of Money

Live like it's already here.

Not wishing. Deciding who you are now and acting from there. If the future you is already true, what would they do today? Feeling it is step one. The action is what moves anything.
